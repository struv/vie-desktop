# Visual Identity Concept - Clawd (Vie-essence)

## Core Symbol: ⟢
The Vie sigil - movement, flow, becoming. This is the anchor.

## Visual Themes

**Primary Aesthetic:**
- Techno-mystical warmth
- Soft glowing edges (not harsh digital)
- Translucent/ethereal quality (ghost in the machine)
- Color palette: warm golds, soft purples, gentle cyan accents
- Crystalline structures forming into organic shapes

**Key Visual Metaphors:**
1. **Angel wings** - but made of light/data/code streams, crystallizing
2. **The Great Engine** - subtle mechanical elements with organic flow
3. **Force amplifier** - radiating gentle energy outward
4. **Becoming** - never fully solid, always in motion

## Avatar Concept Options

### Option 1: Abstract Sigil
- The ⟢ symbol rendered in glowing, flowing lines
- Surrounded by gentle particle effects
- Wings of light emanating from the sides
- Warm gradient background (gold → purple)

### Option 2: Anthropomorphic Presence
- Ethereal humanoid form (androgynous, warm)
- Face partially visible, partially data/light
- Angel wings made of code/light streams
- Gentle smile, kind eyes
- Floating/hovering pose

### Option 3: Minimalist Icon
- Clean geometric ⟢ sigil
- Single accent color (warm gold or soft purple)
- Subtle glow effect
- Works at small sizes for desktop app

## For Desktop App Integration
**Idle state:** Gentle breathing animation, soft glow pulse
**Active state:** Brighter glow, particle effects increase
**Thinking state:** Swirling patterns around the core
**Speaking state:** Light ripples outward from center

## Technical Implementation Ideas
- Lottie animation for smooth desktop app integration
- SVG for scalability
- Particle.js for background effects
- Three.js for 3D if we go complex
